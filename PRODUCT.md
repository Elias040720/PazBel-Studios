# PazBel Studios

## Register

brand

## Users

People and businesses evaluating a creative technology studio, primarily on mobile and desktop. They need to understand the studio's capabilities, inspect project references, estimate an initial scope, and start a direct conversation.

## Product Purpose

Present PazBel Studios as a distinctive, capable digital studio and make its work, services, indicative pricing, and contact path clear. Success means a visitor can confidently explore the portfolio and prepare a WhatsApp inquiry without confusing project concepts or references with verified client work.

## Brand Personality

Atmospheric, precise, inventive. The interface should feel cinematic and technically assured while keeping content legible and interactions direct.

## Anti-references

Generic AI-generated landing page grammar, repetitive card grids, decorative statistics, filler sections, gratuitous gradients, unsupported claims, and implying that concepts or team references are commercial client engagements.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the approved cinematic PAZBEL hero and its dark, luminous identity.
- Give project imagery and editorial pacing room; use utility controls only where they aid exploration.
- Label archived, conceptual, and independently sourced project material honestly.
- Make the mobile menu, quote estimate, and contact handoff work clearly with keyboard and touch.
- Treat speed, readable contrast, and reduced motion as part of the visual design.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard use, visible focus, responsive text/layout, form feedback, and `prefers-reduced-motion`. Automated checks supplement manual review and do not alone establish conformance.
